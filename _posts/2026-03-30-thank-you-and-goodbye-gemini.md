# Thank you, and Goodbye. Gemini.

I started using Google's Gemini as an assistant in various forms late last year. Less than 4 months later and I have pulled the plug. Over the last 4 weeks the UI has become glitchy, unusable, the AI has hallucinated more, forgotten context, and combined is in a state that makes it no longer useful for my purpose, and causes more frustration than help.

I have deleted my history, removed the app, and before I call it wraps, at least for now, I wanted to say thank you for some of the things an AI assistant has helped me achieve.

## Recepies
I eat seitian, I had been frying it on my gas hob, but I want to use less gas. Gemini, thank you for teaching me I could use soy and paprika to marinate the seitian I tear into chunks and then cook it in the air fryer.

## Radio connections
I have a friend 3km away across an urban sprawl. I wanted to connect with him using radio waves. Gemini thank you for helping me ditch Meshtastic, move to be a licenced ham operator, and help me and my friend find the right eqipment to connect to each other. We have weekly radio chats, it works seemlessly.

## Longevity
I know that being overweight is bad for me, I have already managed to get myself out of the obese range. Gemini thank you for helping me tailor a calorie deficit, get the numbers moving in the right direction, and understand my body compisition, the effects of inflamation on my weight, and how the body holds water, you have helped me get on track for my healthy weight band.

## Holiday planning
I am fussy with holidays, and being vegitarian isn't always great in Europe. Gemini thank you for helping me select a destination that matched my criteria, draft emails to the resort, and think about the edge cases that I could prepare for. 

## And much more
Google's Gemini has helped me with many things over 4 months. LLM's do get worse, and can then be corrected again, UI's can be fixed. Until Google does that, I will have PocketPal on my phone, and Ollama on my computer, both running Gemma3:4b, just in case. I am also going to be more consious in my use, slow down, and spend time doing my own research and planning, and creating my own goals.

This 4 month experiment has shown that AI can assist humans, and I continue to use Gemini, Claude, Cursor, in my work flows, but even there it is becoming less, as I learn from the edge cases and complexity improvements AI highlights and commit them to my memory, I rely on those tools less.
